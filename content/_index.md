# Welcome

Cybersecurity. IT infrastructure. Penetration testing.

Welcome to MidwestSec. This site documents my journey through IT and cybersecurity as I continue building skills across offensive security, defensive security, and infrastructure.

I strongly believe that one of the best ways to become a better defender is to understand how attackers think and operate. Learning how systems are attacked has changed the way I approach securing them.

This site is where I document that process.

You'll find walkthroughs, technical notes, projects, lessons learned, and plenty of things that didn't work the first time.

## Why MidwestSec?

I've spent my career working with IT infrastructure, networks, servers, endpoints, and the security controls protecting them.

As I've moved deeper into cybersecurity, I've become increasingly interested in understanding both sides of the equation.

How do we secure a network?

And just as importantly:

How would someone break into it?

Learning offensive security has made me look at defensive security differently. Misconfigurations, excessive permissions, exposed services, weak segmentation, identity issues, and seemingly minor vulnerabilities become much more meaningful once you've seen how they can be chained together during an attack.

That's the idea behind MidwestSec.

It's a place to document what I'm learning, share practical notes and walkthroughs, and continue developing my skills on both sides of cybersecurity.

The goal is simple:

**Become a better defender by learning how to attack.**
